# Report Templates (Basic)

## Daily Stock Summary
- Report period: current day
- Total products
- Low stock items (name, quantity)
- Sales total (sum of sales for the day)

## Weekly Stock Summary
- Report period: Sunday–Saturday
- Total products
- Low stock items (name, quantity)
- Sales total (sum of sales for the week)

## Monthly Stock Summary
- Report period: month to date
- Total products
- Low stock items (name, quantity)
- Sales total (sum of sales for the month)

## Email Subject Lines (Suggestion)
- Daily: `Daily Stock Summary - <YYYY-MM-DD>`
- Weekly: `Weekly Stock Summary - Week of <YYYY-MM-DD>`
- Monthly: `Monthly Stock Summary - <YYYY-MM>`
