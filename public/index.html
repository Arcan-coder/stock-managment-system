Core modules

Every part of the inventory lifecycle is covered in one connected workflow.

Products Suppliers Stock Control Reports Users

Product management

Capture every item with SKU, pricing, supplier, and reorder thresholds so your catalog stays accurate.

  • Add, update, delete, and view products
  • Track cost price, selling price, and profit margins
  • Automatic reorder level warnings

Supplier management

Build a single source of truth for supplier contacts, supplied products, and performance insights.

  • Linked supplier profiles and contact channels
  • Quick access to supplied products
  • Supplier performance scorecards

Stock control

Increase or decrease quantities with every delivery and sale while keeping a full audit trail.

  • Stock in/out movements with timestamps
  • Low-stock alerts and safety stock monitoring
  • Automatic quantity updates after sales

Automated reports

Generate daily, weekly, and monthly summaries that can be exported or sent automatically via email.

  • Sales, stock, supplier, and low-stock reports
  • Downloadable CSV and PDF-ready views
  • Scheduled reporting calendar

User roles & permissions

Control access with admin, manager, and storekeeper roles, including activity logging for accountability.

Admin

Full control over products, users, suppliers, and reports.

Manager

View dashboards and download scheduled reports.

Storekeeper

Update stock movements and receive alerts.
